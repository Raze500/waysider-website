About the Studio

One developer. A clear focus. Apps that just work.

Who we are

Waysider Studio is Graham - a solo independent developer. I build Android apps for everyday use, focused on keeping things simple and actually useful.

No venture capital, no team of 50, no roadmap driven by investor pressure. Just someone who enjoys building things that work well and solving real problems.

Why independent?

Most apps today are bloated, require accounts, push notifications you do not want, and collect data you never agreed to share. I wanted to build the opposite.

Waysider Studio apps are free to use. They work offline where possible. They do not require you to sign up for anything. If they include ads, it is to keep the lights on - not to fund a Series B.

How we build

Simple first

Every feature has to earn its place. If it adds complexity without clear value, it does not ship.

Privacy by default

Offline-first wherever possible. No accounts. No tracking beyond what ads strictly require.

Free to use

Apps are free with optional ads. No paywalls that block core functionality.
