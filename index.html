Philosophy

Why Waysider
feels different.

Radical focus

Each app does one thing. That's it. No hidden tabs, no expert settings, no features "for when you grow". If you need three tools, you download three apps — each perfect at its job.

No dark patterns

No microscopic close buttons. No "free trial (card required)". No notifications you never asked for.

Real privacy

Honest, readable privacy policy. No data I don't need. No selling information to third parties.

Design that matters

"It works" is not enough. It has to look good too. Design is not decoration — it's the difference between an app you use every day and one you uninstall in 3 minutes.
